# Jo's Bot Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 2026**

Jo's Bot is a Discord community management and reputation bot. This Privacy Policy explains what information the bot accesses, what information it stores, how that information is used, and how users may request deletion of stored data.

## 1. Information Jo's Bot Accesses

Jo's Bot may access information provided by Discord that is necessary to operate its features, including:

- Discord user IDs
- Discord server (guild) IDs
- Usernames and server nicknames
- Server membership information
- Discord role information
- Information provided directly through Discord slash commands and interactions

Jo's Bot may also access server member information when necessary to verify membership, determine role eligibility, and manage reputation-based Discord roles.

## 2. Information Jo's Bot Stores

Jo's Bot may store the following information in its database:

- Discord user IDs
- Reputation scores
- Reputation transactions and history
- Timestamps associated with reputation activity
- Reputation cooldown information
- Records required to prevent or identify abuse of the reputation system
- Administrative changes made to reputation data

This information is associated with Discord identifiers so that the bot can provide its reputation and moderation functionality.

## 3. Message Content

Jo's Bot primarily operates using Discord slash commands and interactions.

Jo's Bot does not intentionally collect or store the content of ordinary Discord messages.

## 4. How Information Is Used

Information accessed or stored by Jo's Bot is used only to operate and maintain its features, including:

- Allowing users to give reputation to other server members
- Displaying reputation scores
- Displaying reputation leaderboards and history
- Managing reputation cooldowns
- Preventing and detecting abuse of the reputation system
- Verifying whether users are eligible to use reputation features
- Managing Discord roles associated with reputation levels
- Providing moderator and administrative reputation tools
- Maintaining reputation and moderation logs
- Operating server utility and event-related commands

Information is not used for advertising or behavioural profiling.

## 5. Privileged Discord Data

Jo's Bot may request access to Discord's Server Members privileged gateway intent when required for its functionality.

This access is used to support features that require server-member information, including member verification and reputation-based role management.

Jo's Bot does not use member information for advertising, marketing, profiling, or unrelated analytics.

## 6. Data Sharing

Jo's Bot does not sell personal information.

Information collected by Jo's Bot is not sold, rented, or intentionally disclosed to third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Information may be processed by infrastructure providers necessary to operate the bot, such as Discord, the bot hosting provider, and database hosting services.

## 7. Data Storage and Security

Certain information is stored in a managed database service used by Jo's Bot.

Stored data is encrypted at rest by the database hosting provider, and connections between Jo's Bot and the database use encrypted TLS/SSL connections.

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect database credentials and stored information against unauthorized access.

## 8. Data Retention

Stored data is retained for as long as it is reasonably necessary to provide Jo's Bot's reputation, moderation, and server-management functionality.

Data may also be removed when:

- it is no longer required for the bot's functionality;
- the bot is permanently discontinued;
- a valid deletion request is received; or
- removal is otherwise required by applicable law.

## 9. Data Deletion Requests

Users may request deletion of personal data stored by Jo's Bot.

To identify the relevant records, users may be asked to provide their Discord user ID.

Deletion requests may be submitted to the Jo's Bot owner through the Discord server where the bot is installed or through the contact method listed below.

Some information may be retained where reasonably necessary for security, abuse prevention, legal compliance, or maintaining the integrity of moderation records.

## 10. Discord

Jo's Bot uses Discord's services and API.

Use of Discord is separately governed by Discord's own Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

Jo's Bot only requests Discord permissions and gateway intents that are reasonably necessary for its functionality.

## 11. Children's Privacy

Jo's Bot is intended for use through Discord and is subject to Discord's own age and account requirements.

Jo's Bot does not intentionally collect age information or information specifically intended to identify children.

## 12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if Jo's Bot's functionality, data practices, infrastructure, or Discord requirements change.

The current version of this policy will remain publicly available at this URL.
